Privacy Policy

Debugging data

Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage analytics under its sharing settings and privacy practices. App Store users can choose whether to share diagnostic and usage information with developers; TestFlight users share crash reports with developers automatically. See Apple's crash reporting documentation.

Analytics & usage data

Kilowatt 5.5.0 does not include AppTelemetry or TelemetryDeck analytics integrations. Earlier development versions included analytics integrations, and information from those versions may still be held by the providers. We are checking the status and retention of historical records; please contact us with questions or requests about them.

Purchase history

Earlier development versions included an in-app donation integration using RevenueCat, which could record the amount, an anonymous identifier, and the date and time. Kilowatt 5.5.0 does not include that integration. Historical records may still be held by the provider; we are checking their status and retention. Please contact us with questions or requests about them.

Personal data

Kilowatt stores vehicle, charger and charging-session information on your device, and shares the information needed by its widget through an app group. If you contact us, we can see your email address and any information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request.

Website analytics

This website uses Simple Analytics to understand website usage. For details of the information the service collects, see Simple Analytics' data collection documentation.

Any questions?

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
